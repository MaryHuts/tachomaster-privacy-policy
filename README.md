    <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
    <h2>Tachomaster</h2>  
    <p>MaryHut respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy describes what information is collected and used while using the <strong>Tachomaster</strong> mobile application.</p>
    <p><strong>Effective Date:</strong> April 16, 2026</p>

    <h3>1. Educational Purpose</h3>
    <p><strong>Tachomaster</strong> is an educational simulation designed to help users understand transport regulations, driver work-time rules, and tachograph operations. It is intended for students and logistics professionals.</p>

    <h3>2. Data Collection and Usage</h3>
    <h4>Personal Data:</h4>  
    <p>Tachomaster <strong>does not collect</strong> personal data (such as names, addresses, or phone numbers).</p>

    <h4>Simulation Data:</h4>  
    <p>Data related to simulated driving times and rest periods are processed via <strong>Lovable / Supabase</strong> to synchronize the state between the instructor (Guide) and the player. This data is temporary and non-identifiable.</p>

    <h4>No Direct Communication:</h4>
    <p>The application <strong>does not include a chat system</strong> or any form of direct messaging between players. Interaction is strictly confined to simulation-based inputs.</p>

    <h3>3. Technical Analytics:</h3>
    <p>Developed with <strong>Lovable</strong>, the app may automatically record anonymous diagnostic data (crash logs, performance stats) to ensure the simulation runs smoothly on all devices.</p>
