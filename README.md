# Privacy Policy - Tachomaster

MaryHut respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy describes what information is collected and used while using the **Tachomaster** mobile application.

Effective Date: April 16, 2026

## 1. Educational Purpose

**Tachomaster** is an educational simulation designed to help users understand transport regulations, driver work-time rules, and tachograph operations. It is intended for students and logistics professionals.

## 2. Data Collection and Usage

Personal Data:

Tachomaster **does not collect** personal data (such as names, addresses, or phone numbers).

Simulation Data:

Data related to simulated driving times and rest periods are processed via **Lovable / Supabase**. This data is temporary and non-identifiable.

No Direct Communication:

The application **does not include a chat system** or any form of direct messaging between players. Interaction is strictly confined to simulation-based inputs.

## 3. Technical Analytics:

Developed with **Lovable**, the app may automatically record anonymous diagnostic data (crash logs, performance stats) to ensure the simulation runs smoothly on all devices.
